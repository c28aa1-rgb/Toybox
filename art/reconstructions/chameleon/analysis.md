# Chameleon: image analysis

Reference: 1254x1254 AI concept, 3/4 front view, neutral grey background.

## Layer 1: identification
Stylized vinyl-toy mascot, `primaryDomain: character`, confidence 0.95.

## Layer 2: form & silhouette
Egg body (head and torso one volume, ~1.33 tall:wide), two teardrop mitts at the sides, two violet
feet, plus the accessories below. Bilateral symmetry except the accessories and the raised mitt.

## Layer 3-4: decomposition and relationships
Macro: body, mitt x2, foot x2. Meso: eyes x2. Micro: pupils, catchlights.
- `eye-socket-l/r`: mint turret bulges the eyes sit in, standing proud of the head like a real chameleon.
- `crest` + `crest-2`: rounded mint fin on the crown leaning back, with a smaller bump behind it.
- `tail`: thick mint tail coiled into a flat spiral on the character-left side behind the hanging mitt, ~0.5 m across.
- Cream belly ellipse, large and low; ~12 lighter-mint spots scattered over the crown, flanks and tail.

## Layer 5-6: materials and colour
Glossy dielectric plastic everywhere (metalness 0, roughness ~0.25). Body mint green, mid-high value (~#3DDBB4).
Value changes across each part are studio shading, not paint.

## Layer 7: identity features
1. Curly spiral tail. 2. Eye turrets. 3. Crown crest. 4. Lighter spots. 5. Cream belly.

## Layer 8: single-view limits
Tail root and the back of the coil are hidden behind the body (assumed to join the lower back). Spot layout on the back is inferred.
