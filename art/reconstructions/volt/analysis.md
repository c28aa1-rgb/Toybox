# Volt: image analysis

Reference: 1254x1254 AI concept, 3/4 front view, neutral grey background.

## Layer 1: identification
Stylized vinyl-toy mascot, `primaryDomain: character`, confidence 0.95.

## Layer 2: form & silhouette
Egg body (head and torso one volume, ~1.33 tall:wide), two teardrop mitts at the sides, two violet
feet, plus the accessories below. Bilateral symmetry except the accessories and the raised mitt.

## Layer 3-4: decomposition and relationships
Macro: body, mitt x2, foot x2. Meso: eyes x2. Micro: pupils, catchlights.
- Body is an upright rounded cylinder (an AA battery), not an egg: ~1.8x taller than wide, flat top with soft shoulders.
- `cap-base` + `cap-button`: silver plus-terminal cap on the crown, a flat ring and a raised button.
- Purple band painted round the lower quarter of the body (the battery wrapper).
- `bolt`: flat white lightning bolt on the belly, slightly raised.
- `cable-1..3` + `plug` + `prong-a/b`: cream charging cable curling out from behind the hanging mitt, ending in a purple plug with two silver prongs.

## Layer 5-6: materials and colour
Glossy dielectric plastic everywhere (metalness 0, roughness ~0.25). Body lemon yellow, high value (~#FFE45C).
Value changes across each part are studio shading, not paint.

## Layer 7: identity features
1. Battery body with silver cap. 2. Lightning bolt. 3. Plug tail. 4. Purple band. 5. Big eyes.

## Layer 8: single-view limits
Cable root hidden behind the mitt (assumed to leave the lower back). Back of the cap assumed round.
